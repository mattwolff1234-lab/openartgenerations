# Barbershop Revenge Reel: Higgsfield Recreation Pack

Source: 62s, 1:1 (720x720), Pixar-style 3D animation, ~30 shots, burned-in word-by-word captions.
Story: cocky regular flexes his viral AI videos on the barber. Barber learns the tool overnight, grinds, blows up, and ends up next to him at a red light in a supercar.

**@Avatar** = your avatar (replaces the curly-haired barber, the hero).
**@Rival** = the cocky customer (backwards black cap, brown tee, diamond stud). Generate him once and save him as an Element so he stays consistent.
**[PRODUCT]** = whatever the hero uses to make his videos. In the original it's getpixmo.com, so swap in your own.

---

## Setup in Higgsfield (do this first)

1. **Avatar sheet.** Upload one clear photo of your avatar and save it as an Element (`@Avatar`). If it's a real person, first generate a Pixar-style version so it matches the look:
   > 3D animated Pixar-style character portrait of @Avatar, same face and hairstyle, big expressive eyes, soft subsurface skin, white ringer t-shirt with black trim, neutral gray studio background, front-facing, soft key light
2. **Rival.** Generate and save as an Element (`@Rival`):
   > 3D animated Pixar-style character, confident young man, light-brown skin, sharp jawline, backwards black snapback, small diamond stud earring, fitted brown t-shirt, smug half-smile, neutral gray background, front-facing
3. **Model:** Seedance 2.0 (native audio + dialogue, multi-shot, takes Elements). Fallback: Cinema Studio Video 3.0. Aspect 1:1 to match the original, or 9:16 for Reels.
4. Generate the five clips below, then stitch them and add word-by-word white captions (bold, glowing, centered at eye level).

**Style block (put at the top of every clip):**
> 3D animated feature-film style, Pixar/Disney look, big expressive eyes, soft subsurface skin, warm cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, smooth camera moves, rich color, crisp detail, natural sound design.
