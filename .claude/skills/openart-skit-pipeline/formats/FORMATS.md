# Story Formats

Each video uses ONE format. Never repeat the format a creator used last time (check their previous runs; if unknown, ask once). Within a format, change the names, props, lines and demo topic every time.

Every format keeps the same 5-clip skeleton so the pipeline, timings and edit templates stay the same:

| Clip | Len | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | 15s | Jarring prop in the first second, the doubter's cut line, ends on the hero holding the prop |
| 2 Grind | 12s | Opens on the same prop, a montage of making explainers, ends with the prop "resolved"; edit card "X LATER" |
| 3 Reveal | 15s | Status flip in front of the doubters, a callback line, then "How did you...?" → "Five steps." |
| 4 Lesson | 15s | Office/desk at night, 5 OpenArt Explainer steps, screen-recording cards |
| 5 CTA | 8s | Comment keyword (+ optional coach-approved money beat), `openart.ai/try` on screen |

Cast roles use these tags for auto-casting (see `prompts/cast_from_hero.txt`):
- **CONTRAST**: must look clearly different from the hero (other hair colour/texture, build, face shape, palette).
- **FAMILY**: must visibly resemble the hero (same skin tone, eye shape, nose, hair colour) but older/younger with their own distinct style.

---

## F1. Evicted (proven)
- **Cast:** Partner (CONTRAST, creator picks palette) · Rival with a "real job" (CONTRAST, lanyard + vest) · Landlord (forearm + fist only, no sheet)
- **Sets:** apartment door + hallway · bedroom desk at night · sunny café · upscale office at night
- **Hook prop:** red eviction notice slapped on the door
- **Cut line:** "Go make your little cartoons."
- **Grind ends:** crumples the notice ("Rent's paid.")
- **Reveal:** picks up their tab at the café; badge-free sports car outside; callback "Cartoons."

## F2. Fired
- **Cast:** Boss (CONTRAST, older, sharp suit, reading glasses on a chain) · Smug coworker (CONTRAST, gelled hair, company fleece) · Security guard (CONTRAST, background only, optional)
- **Sets:** glass-walled office · cubicle row · bedroom desk at night · company lobby with a big screen · upscale office at night
- **Hook prop:** a cardboard box slammed onto his desk ("Clear your desk.")
- **Cut line (boss):** "Anyone could do your job with a cartoon."
- **Grind ends:** he puts his old company lanyard in the cardboard box and closes it.
- **Reveal:** the lobby screen plays his explainer; the boss asks his assistant "Who made this? Get them on the phone." Hero walks past, visitor badge, calm smirk; callback "Just a cartoon."

## F3. Family Dinner Roast
- **Cast:** Mom (FAMILY, warm but disappointed) · Dad (FAMILY, stern, reading glasses) · Golden-child cousin (FAMILY, med-school scrubs or a blazer)
- **Sets:** crowded family dining table · hero's childhood bedroom desk at night · the same dining table, holiday decorations · upscale office at night
- **Hook prop:** a framed photo of the cousin's graduation shoved in front of his plate
- **Cut line (dad):** "Cartoons don't pay bills, beta." (use the family's language/term of endearment the creator gives)
- **Grind ends:** he props up a small frame of his own first video thumbnail next to the cousin's photo
- **Reveal:** next holiday, the cousin's kid is watching his explainer on a tablet at the table; Dad goes quiet; callback "Cartoons, Dad." Cousin: "How did you...?"
- Note: keep it loving-roast, not cruel; Mom gets a proud beat.

## F4. Friends Laughed
- **Cast:** Loud best friend (CONTRAST, bucket hat) · Friend with the new car (CONTRAST, polo shirt) · Friend's partner (CONTRAST, optional)
- **Sets:** backyard barbecue or house party · bedroom desk at night · same backyard, a year later · upscale office at night
- **Hook prop:** a phone held up to his face playing his old, bad video while everyone laughs
- **Cut line:** "Bro, nobody's watching that."
- **Grind ends:** the same phone (his) buzzing nonstop on the desk with notifications (no readable text)
- **Reveal:** at next year's party the friends' kids are glued to his explainer; best friend: "Wait, that's YOU?"; callback "Nobody's watching, right?"

## F5. Stood Up
- **Cast:** Date (CONTRAST, creator picks palette) · Waiter (CONTRAST, bow tie, background)
- **Sets:** fancy restaurant table for two · bedroom desk at night · rooftop event or creator meetup · upscale office at night
- **Hook prop:** his phone lighting up on the white tablecloth next to two candles (cancellation text; cover the screen with a caption in the edit)
- **Cut line (her voice note, spoken):** "Call me when you're actually somebody."
- **Grind ends:** he blows out a candle next to the laptop and gets back to work
- **Reveal:** she spots him at a rooftop event surrounded by people asking for pictures; she says "Hey... stranger."; callback (he answers his ringing phone with a smirk): "I'm somebody now. I'll call you."

## F6. Reunion
- **Cast:** Class bully, now a car salesman (CONTRAST, slick hair, cheap suit) · Former crush (CONTRAST) · Old teacher (CONTRAST, older, cardigan)
- **Sets:** high-school hallway with lockers · bedroom desk at night · reunion gym with balloons · upscale office at night
- **Hook prop:** a yearbook slammed open to his photo captioned "Most Likely To... Nothing" (caption added in the edit)
- **Cut line (bully):** "Most likely to nothing. Still true?"
- **Grind ends:** he tapes the yearbook photo above his desk, then turns it face-down
- **Reveal:** at the reunion the teacher is playing his explainer for her class on her phone; crush: "How did you...?"; callback "Most likely to... this."

---

## Writing a new format

Keep the skeleton and the CTA. Change: who doubts him, where, the hook prop (physical, readable in one second, no violence), the cut line (one sentence, quotable), how the prop resolves in the grind, and the reveal (a status flip they see with their own eyes, with no income numbers unless the coach approved). The callback in clip 3 reuses words from the cut line.
